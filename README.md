> **Track 1:** *Same Baby, 1,000 Websites — Pip the Skull Baby*  
> **Tagline:** *“I build the dreams you fall asleep inside.”*

---

## 🌟 Overview & Character Profile

- **Name:** Pip, The Skull Baby
- **Species & Appearance:** A sleepy, gentle baby with a smooth round skull face, huge soft dark eye sockets with warm glowing amber pinpricks of light, a tiny crooked smile, and a bedtime nightcap adorned with stars and a little pink bow. *(Always cute, cozy, and friendly — never scary, gory, or creepy!)*
- **Personality:** Curious, clumsy, sleepy, easily delighted. Giggles at everything and hates silence.
- **Profession:** Junior Dream Engineer at Midnight Nursery.
- **Craft:** Pip designs the dreams, lullabies, nightlights, and bedtime stories that help the neighborhood's little creatures fall asleep safely.

## 🚀 Showcase Projects (5 Implemented — Exceeds 3-Project Minimum!)

1. **The Lullaby Lab (Web Audio Synthesizer):**
   - Procedural pentatonic music box synthesizer crafted with native Web Audio API oscillators (no broken audio links!).
   - Interactive winding ratchet key with physics animation.
   - Real-time frequency waveform visualizer on HTML5 canvas.
   - 3 melody presets (*Midnight Nursery*, *Starlight Waltz*, *Rooftop Drift*) + playable pentatonic chime keyboard (C5, D5, E5, G5, A5, C6).
2. **The Nightlight Collection (Dynamic Ambient Illumination):**
   - Clickable bedside lanterns (Crescent Moon Lamp, Firefly Glass Orb, Stardust Mushroom).
   - Clicking a lamp dynamically adjusts the room's ambient illumination and color hue!
3. **Dream Delivery Routes (Interactive SVG Rooftop Flight Map):**
   - Animated SVG nocturnal flight route across chimney tops and starry clouds.
   - Clickable waypoints (*The Burrow*, *Kitten Crib*, *Cloud Nest*, *Pip's Base*) displaying delivery manifests and bedtime stories.
4. **Playable Wooden Nursery Blocks:**
   - 3D wooden blocks stamped with **H-A-X** and celestial symbols. Tapping them rattles and tumbles them with custom wooden percussion audio.
5. **Pip's Growth Chart:**
   - Nursery wall height ruler tracking Pip's milestones from first baby giggle to solo rooftop flight certification.
